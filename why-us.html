Product Performance

What makes K-Flex insulation perform

Technical characteristics of the K-Flex elastomeric range we supply.

Closed-cell structure

Stable, very low thermal conductivity; smooth skin gives high water-vapour resistance and consistent performance.

Low thermal conductivity

As low as 0.033 W/(m·K) at 0 °C for long-term efficiency and energy saving.

Built-in vapour barrier

μ value above 10,000 — a separate vapour barrier is not required on K-Flex products.

Fire behaviour

Class "O" to BS 476 Parts 6 & 7, FM Global and UL 94 approved; self-extinguishing, no flaming droplets.

Flexible & easy to fit

Fast installation on ducts, pipes, elbows, valves, flanges and complex shapes.

Quality & safety

Certified by major international laboratories; meets norms for marine, rail, oil & gas and clean rooms.

Wide temperature range

For applications from –200 °C to 175 °C: cryogenics, cold storage, pharma, plumbing and hot water.

Environment friendly

Fibre-free, dust-free, CFC/HCFC-free; does not support bacteria, mould or fungi; no VOC/SVOC emission.

Beyond Thermal

Acoustic, roofing and waterproofing too

  • K-Fonik open-cell acoustic foam — fibre-free, with Ultra-Fresh® antimicrobial protection, for duct lining, AHU rooms and DG set enclosures.
  • Insu Board XPS — high compressive strength and water absorption below 1% by volume, for roofs, walls, floors and cold stores.
  • Insu Shield — double-side aluminium foil faced roof insulation for PEB sheds, acting as radiant and vapour barrier.
  • Water stoppers, swellable bars & geotextile — for basements, tanks and ground works.
