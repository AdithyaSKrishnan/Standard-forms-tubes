About Us

A Calicut distributor built around insulation

Located at AM Complex near Cherooty Road, Calicut, Standard Forms & Tubes supplies contractors, HVAC firms, builders, industries and interior specialists with branded insulation and civil accessories.

  • K-Flex closed-cell sheets & tubes and K-Fonik acoustic foam
  • Supreme XPS boards, PEB roof insulation, backer rods & joint fillers
  • Acoustic treatment for theatres, auditoriums, studios & conference rooms
