About Us

Branded insulation, acoustic and civil accessory materials — distributed from Calicut.

Who We Are

Standard Forms & Tubes

Standard Forms & Tubes is a Calicut-based distributor of K-Flex Insulations and the Supreme Civil Accessories Division. We supply high-performance insulation systems and the accessories that complete them to HVAC contractors, builders, industries, architects and interior firms.

Our range covers closed-cell elastomeric insulation for air-conditioning, refrigeration and process piping; XPS boards and reflective insulation for roofs, walls and floors; acoustic materials for theatres, auditoriums and studios; and civil accessories such as backer rods, joint fillers, water stoppers and geotextiles.

Because we work with these materials every day, we can help customers choose the right product, thickness and finish for the job — and supply the tapes, adhesives and accessories needed to install it properly.
